QR Ordering

Scan. Order. Pay.

Guests scan a QR code on their table and order directly from their phone — no app download required, or as a fully branded native app.

  • Table-side QR code ordering
  • Live digital menu with photos & modifiers
  • Send orders straight to kitchen / POS
  • Apple Pay, Google Pay & card checkout
  • Order status tracking for guests
  • Fully white-labelled to restaurant brand
Rewards & Membership

  • Points earned on every order & visit
  • Bronze, Silver, Gold membership tiers
  • Birthday rewards & personalised offers
  • Push notifications for promos & events
  • Digital stamp cards & referral bonuses
  • Member-only menu items & early access
Voice AI

Your restaurant's AI host — always on, always booking.

Visitors click "Try our AI assistant" and have a real voice conversation. The AI asks party size, date, and time — then books the table and shows a confirmation popup on screen.

  • Natural voice conversation via browser — no app download
  • Table booking with 6-character confirmation code
  • Answers hours, location, and menu questions
  • Integrates with restaurant opening hours & availability API
  • Works on both Cinematic and Aurora templates
